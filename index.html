Chalq

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Chalq is a classroom announcement app developed independently by CodeWithPhyl. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information depending on your role:

  • Teachers: Full name and email address, used for authentication.
  • Students: Device ID and push notification token. No name or email is collected from students.
  • Usage data: Which announcements have been read, and class membership records.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate teachers and manage their accounts.
  • To deliver class announcements to enrolled students.
  • To send push notifications when new announcements are posted.
  • To automatically categorize announcements using AI (title and message text only).

3. Third-Party Services

Chalq uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and backend hosting. Stores teacher accounts, class data, and announcements.
  • Expo Push Notifications — push notification delivery. Receives device push tokens and notification content.
  • HiveMQ — real-time messaging. Receives announcement content in transit via MQTT protocol.
  • Groq — AI classification. Receives announcement title and message text to automatically assign a category. No data is stored by Groq.

4. Data Retention

  • Announcements expire automatically after 7 days.
  • Teacher accounts and all associated class data are retained until the account is deleted.
  • Students can leave a class at any time, which removes their device ID and push token from our records.

5. Account Deletion

Teachers can permanently delete their account directly within the app:

  • Go to Preferences → Delete Account and confirm with your password.
  • Deletion is immediate and permanent. The following data is removed: your teacher account, all classes you created, all announcements, all student membership records, and all push tokens associated with your class.
  • This action cannot be undone.

Alternatively, you may request account deletion by emailing us at officialchalqapp@gmail.com. We will process email deletion requests within 7 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Chalq is intended for use in educational settings under teacher supervision. The app is designed for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us so we can remove their data.

7. Data Security

Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth using industry-standard security practices. Student data is limited to device identifiers only — no names or emails are collected from students. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS).

8. Your Rights

Teachers can delete their account and all associated data at any time from within the app. For any other data requests or concerns, contact us at officialchalqapp@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact
